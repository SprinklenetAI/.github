Sprinklenet builds and operates AI applications for enterprise and government, and our customers own their intelligence. The applications run on Knowledge Spaces, our managed service for AI governance and knowledge management. Customers choose fully managed operation or their own environment.

We are a U.S. company and a GSA Multiple Award Schedule contract holder, working with enterprises, financial institutions, and government agencies.

## Knowledge Spaces

[Knowledge Spaces](https://sprinklenet.com/knowledge-spaces/) governs how AI applications reach an organization's data and foundation models:

- More than 16 foundation models behind one governed interface
- Four-tier role-based access, scoped per space and per bot
- Retrieval limited to the sources each bot is granted
- A guardrail engine with PII detection, prompt-injection prevention, and content moderation
- An audit trail that records more than 40 event types
- Fully managed by Sprinklenet, or deployed in the customer's own AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud account, including government regions

Read the [Knowledge Spaces white paper](https://sprinklenet.com/knowledge-spaces-white-paper/) for the architecture, the [security white paper](https://sprinklenet.com/knowledge-spaces-security/) for the governance and data-protection model, and the rest of our [white papers](https://sprinklenet.com/papers/).

## Applications

We build assistants, agentic workflows, and decision dashboards on Knowledge Spaces for a specific team's work, including Merlin for AI governance, Falcon, MASTYF, Knowledge Spaces VRMS for third-party and vendor risk, and AB HUB. Every application inherits the platform's access control, model routing, evaluation, and audit trail.
