## Knowledge Spaces

[Knowledge Spaces](https://sprinklenet.com/knowledge-spaces/) is our flagship platform. It turns unstructured document repositories into governed, conversational knowledge systems:

- Multi-LLM orchestration across 16+ foundation models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Groq, xAI, and others)
- Retrieval-augmented generation with vector search
- Role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, and exportable audit logging
- A guardrail engine with PII detection, prompt-injection prevention, and content moderation
- Deployment on AWS, Azure, GCP, on-premises, or air-gapped, with GovCloud-ready paths

Read the [Knowledge Spaces white paper](https://sprinklenet.com/knowledge-spaces-white-paper/) for the full architecture and approach, the [security white paper](https://sprinklenet.com/knowledge-spaces-security/) for the governance and data-protection model, and the rest of our [white papers](https://sprinklenet.com/papers/).
